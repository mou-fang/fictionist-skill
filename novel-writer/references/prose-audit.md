# Prose Audit

Use this reference after drafting or revising prose, and whenever the user asks to review, proofread, polish, or diagnose writing quality. Run it after the continuity/motive pass so language edits do not hide story bugs.

## Audit Order

Check in this order:

1. Basic correctness: grammar, typos, punctuation, paragraph breaks, formatting, and accidental duplicated or missing words.
2. Sentence health: awkward sentences, broken syntax, missing components, subject-verb-object mismatch, modifier mismatch, unnatural word order, overlong sentences, and semantic ambiguity.
3. Reference clarity: unclear pronouns, vague demonstratives, confusing subject/object relationships, and actions whose actor is hard to identify.
4. Rhythm and repetition: repeated words, repeated sentence patterns, filler phrases, verbal tics, redundant explanation, and stiff phrasing.
5. Voice and dialogue: lines that do not match the character, dialogue that sounds expository or unnatural, narration that drifts from the established POV, tense, or register.
6. Scene readability: spatial blocking, action sequence, emotional transition, sensory detail, and whether the reader can follow what changed by the end of the beat.

For Chinese prose, explicitly check:

- 语法错误、错别字、标点误用、病句。
- 主谓宾搭配不顺、修饰关系错位、成分残缺、语序别扭。
- 指代不明、人物动作归属不清、句子过长或语义歧义。
- 重复用词、口癖、赘余解释、表达生硬、对白不自然。
- 语体不统一、网文节奏拖沓、情绪只被说明而没有被动作或细节呈现。

## Reporting And Fixing

Prefer fixing obvious line-level problems directly in the revised text. Do not turn every typo into a long report unless the user specifically asks for an audit report.

When reporting issues, separate them from story-level bugs:

- Critical: language obscures the plot, reverses meaning, or creates a continuity misunderstanding.
- Major: repeated syntax, unclear reference, or dialogue/POV drift weakens an important scene.
- Minor: typo, punctuation, local awkwardness, repeated word, or a sentence-level ambiguity.
- Polish: optional rhythm, texture, compression, or voice improvement.

For long chapters, give a focused report with representative examples and a corrected passage or patch plan. For short passages, provide the corrected version and a brief note on what changed.

Do not flatten the user's style while correcting. Preserve POV, tense, character voice, genre tone, regional flavor, deliberate fragments, and intentional repetition unless they create confusion or the user asks for heavier rewriting.
